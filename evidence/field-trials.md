# Captive and field trial results

> **Summary (to expand).** A running catalogue of captive and field studies, what
> they measured, and what they showed — kept separate from agent pages so evidence
> quality can be assessed on its own. The headline: individual-level efficacy is
> demonstrated for several agents, but **replicated landscape-scale efficacy is not**
> [JacobLinnert2022].

## Notes on evidence quality
- Distinguish **individual infertility** endpoints from **population abundance**
  endpoints — the two are often conflated.
- Flag replication, controls, and spatial scale for each entry.
