# Baiting, uptake, and non-target exposure

> **Summary (to expand).** Delivery is the make-or-break constraint for rodent
> fertility control: an agent only works if a large enough fraction of breeders
> consume enough of it, repeatedly, while limiting exposure to non-target species.

## Delivery formats
- Liquid bait stations (e.g. ContraPest)
- Solid/grain baits (steroid baits like EP-1)
- Oral vaccine baits (immunocontraception — technically hardest)

## Uptake challenges
- Competition with abundant natural food in urban/agricultural settings
- Bait shyness / neophobia
- Achieving the coverage fraction required by
  [population models](../ecology-and-modelling/population-dynamics.md)
- Re-dosing interval to sustain effect

## Non-target exposure & residues
- Which non-target species access baits; residue persistence (e.g. triptolide,
  steroids) [Vole2024]
- Secondary exposure through predation/scavenging
