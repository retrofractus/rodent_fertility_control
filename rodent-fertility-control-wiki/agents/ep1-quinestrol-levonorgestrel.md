# EP-1 — quinestrol + levonorgestrel

> **Summary (to expand).** EP-1 is a **synthetic steroid combination** (quinestrol,
> an estrogen, + levonorgestrel, a progestin) delivered in bait. Unlike
> [ContraPest](contrapest-vcd-triptolide.md), it acts after only a **few days** of
> intake, and it has been trialled across many species and countries. **Registered
> in Tanzania** for multimammate mice [Massei2024].

## Composition & mechanism
- Quinestrol (long-acting estrogen) + levonorgestrel (progestin)
- Disrupts the hormonal cycle, implantation, and gametogenesis in both sexes

## Evidence
- Short dosing window (~3–7 days) vs. weeks for VCD/triptolide [Massei2024]
- Tested in China, Tanzania, Zambia, Ethiopia, Indonesia (captive and/or field)
  [JacobLinnert2022]
- Species studies: Pacific rat *Rattus exulans* [LiuEP1-2024]; Brandt's vole
  *Lasiopodomys brandtii* [BrandtVole2020]

## Regulatory status
- Registered in Tanzania for *Mastomys natalensis*; status varies by country
  → [Regulatory status](../regulation/regulatory-status.md)

## Open questions
- Environmental fate of steroid residues; non-target and ecotoxicology
- Landscape-scale efficacy and coverage requirements
