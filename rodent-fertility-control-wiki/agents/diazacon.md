# DiazaCon (20,25-diazacholesterol)

> **Summary (to expand).** DiazaCon inhibits cholesterol conversion, disrupting
> synthesis of cholesterol-derived steroid hormones needed for reproduction.
> Trialled in prairie dogs and other species; **no longer registered in the USA**
> [Massei2024].

## Mechanism
- Blocks the conversion of desmosterol to cholesterol → depresses steroidogenesis

## Evidence
- Bait placed near prairie dog burrows for ~10 days reduced the proportion of young
  over ~3 months [Massei2024]

## Status
- Not currently registered for use in the USA [Massei2024]

## Open questions
- Non-target effects (cholesterol pathway is not reproduction-specific)
