# Regulatory status by jurisdiction

> **Summary (to expand).** Very few fertility control products have achieved
> registration anywhere, which is itself a key finding [JacobLinnert2022]. Verify
> current labels/registrations against primary regulator databases before relying
> on anything here.

## United States (EPA / FIFRA)
- **ContraPest** — EPA-registered contraceptive for Norway and roof rats. Regulatory
  characterization (full registration vs. 25(b) minimum-risk) has varied across
  product versions — **verify the current label**. See
  [ContraPest](../agents/contrapest-vcd-triptolide.md).
- **DiazaCon** — not currently registered in the USA [Massei2024].

## Tanzania / Africa
- **EP-1** — registered in Tanzania for *Mastomys natalensis* [Massei2024].

## China and others
- Various steroid baits used/trialled; registration status varies.

## GMO / self-disseminating agents
- Virally vectored immunocontraception faces distinct biosafety and GMO regulatory
  hurdles. See [Immunocontraception](../agents/immunocontraception.md).
