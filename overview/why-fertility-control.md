# Why fertility control? Rationale vs. lethal control

> **Summary (to expand).** The case for fertility control (humaneness, reduced
> secondary poisoning, potential species-specificity, avoiding compensatory
> rebound) set against the counterarguments (unproven at scale, cost, delivery
> difficulty, reversibility).

## The case for
- Humaneness and public acceptance [Massei2024]
- Reduced non-target/secondary poisoning vs. anticoagulants
- Potential species-specificity
- Avoids lethal control's compensatory survival/reproduction rebound

## The case against / open challenges
- Not yet demonstrated at landscape scale [JacobLinnert2022]
- Continuous delivery cost and logistics
- Reversibility → maintenance tool, not a knockdown
- Residue and non-target questions remain
