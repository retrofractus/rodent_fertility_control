# Glossary

- **Chemosterilant** — a chemical that induces sterility (permanent or long-lasting).
- **Contraceptive** — an agent that prevents conception/implantation, usually reversible.
- **EBRM** — Ecologically Based Rodent Management; landscape-scale management using
  knowledge of rodent ecology.
- **GnRH** — gonadotropin-releasing hormone; master regulator of the reproductive axis
  and a common immunocontraception target.
- **Immunocontraception** — inducing the immune system to attack a reproductive target.
- **IPM** — Integrated Pest Management.
- **Ovotoxicant** — a substance toxic to ovarian follicles (e.g. VCD).
- **TP / triptolide** — antifertility diterpenoid from *Tripterygium wilfordii*.
- **VCD** — 4-vinylcyclohexene diepoxide; ovarian follicle–depleting ovotoxicant.
- **VVIC** — virally vectored immunocontraception.
- **Zona pellucida (ZP)** — glycoprotein coat of the oocyte; ZP3 is a contraception target.
